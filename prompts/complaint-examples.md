# Complaint Letter Examples

## Example 1 — Sample Code Enforcement Notice Of Violation Template

To:  
[Name of the Property Owner]  
[Property Owner’s Address]  
[City, State, ZIP Code]  

From:  
[Name of the Code Enforcement Officer]  
[Department Name]  
[Contact Information]  

Date:  
[Date of Notice]  

Subject:  
Notice of Violation – [Type of Violation] at [Property Address]  

Introduction:  
This notice serves to inform you that your property located at [Property Address] is in violation of [Specify Code/Ordinance] as observed on [Date of Inspection].  

Description of Violation:  
The following specific violations have been noted:  
1. [Detailed description of the violation] (e.g., Unpermitted construction, Issue with zoning, etc.)  
2. [Additional violations, if applicable].  

Required Actions:  
You are required to take corrective actions to resolve the violations listed above by [Compliance Deadline]. Please provide a written response outlining how you plan to address these issues.  

Powers of Enforcement:  
Failure to comply with this notice may result in legal actions, fines, and further enforcement measures as allowed by [State/Local Law].  

Right to Appeal:  
You have the right to appeal this notice. To do so, please submit a written appeal to [Appeals Department] within [Number of Days] days from the date of this notice.  

Contact Information:  
If you have any questions or require further information, please contact [Name] at [Phone Number] or [Email Address].  

Sincerely,  

[Signature of the Code Enforcement Officer]  
[Name of the Code Enforcement Officer]  
[Title]

As of today, the following items need to be repaired:  

______________________________________________________________________________  
______________________________________________________________________________  
______________________________________________________________________________  
______________________________________________________________________________.  

Please contact me by ____________ to schedule the repairs. The best way to reach me is by ________________ at ________________. I would ask you to please follow the notice requirements in our lease and provide me with advance notice of any repair.  

I understand that I have the right to request a housing inspection from the Department of Consumer and Regulatory Affairs (“DCRA”), and that I can bring a lawsuit in D.C. Superior Court for a court order to make the repairs and for money damages. I understand that I may be entitled to a rent abatement for rent that I have already paid to you. I am also aware that it is unlawful for a landlord to retaliate against a tenant for seeking repairs by trying to evict the tenant, raising the rent, or engaging in other prohibited actions.
